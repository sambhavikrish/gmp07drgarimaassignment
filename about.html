About us

Our mission

To help people in rural areas build sustainable businesses using the skills, crops and crafts already present in their communities.

Our vision

Villages where young people and women can earn a living close to home, and where local enterprise keeps money in the local economy.

Why it matters

Rural areas often lack access to credit, training and markets. Many capable entrepreneurs never start because nobody shows them the first step.

How we work

We listen to the community, identify local opportunities, train aspiring entrepreneurs, and connect them with funding and buyers.
