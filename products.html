Products and services

Everything we offer is built for first-time entrepreneurs in rural areas.

Entrepreneurship workshops

Hands-on sessions on business planning, pricing, bookkeeping and customer handling.

Funding guidance

Help with loan applications, self-help group credit and government scheme paperwork.

Market linkage

Introductions to wholesalers, local shops, fairs and online marketplaces.

Mentorship

One-to-one support from experienced business owners during the first year.

Digital skills

Training in UPI payments, social media selling and basic product photography.

Village-made products

A catalogue of handicrafts, food products and farm goods made by our entrepreneurs.
